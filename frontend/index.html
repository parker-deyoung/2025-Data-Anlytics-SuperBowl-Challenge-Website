Key Findings
01 — The Celebrity Flop

Celebrity-driven tweets averaged 81% lower engagement efficiency than organic fan content (WES 114.8 vs. 611.4). Brands with participatory mechanics — DraftKings, Blue Square — achieved 4× the efficiency of celebrity-led campaigns at a fraction of the talent cost.

02 — The 5-Minute Window

88.6% of all tweets concentrated in a single hour during peak game play. A brand has at most a 5-minute window to capture the conversation before the next ad displaces it entirely.

03 — Volume Is Not Value

Ro led all brands in tweet volume (1,861 tweets) yet ranked 16th of 20 in engagement quality. Blue Square generated 28.4× more engagement per tweet than Pepsi Zero Sugar from fewer total tweets. The metric that matters is intensity, not volume.

04 — The Narrative Hijack

Lay's top associated phrase was 'anti-American' — not a tagline or product attribute. Its $8M investment purchased a geopolitical liability. Traditional volume-based scorecards are structurally incapable of detecting this risk.
